## Decisions

| 2026-11-13 | Opened on the owner's request after #9's E2: the scalable path is migrating guest services to parallel host implementations, ranked by gain; the draw side first. On the fork for now |

| 2026-11-13 | **M0 measured; the order holds.** J3D first; background collision second and larger than estimated (the lava room's whole game at 43 %); audio third as estimated; particles fourth, but 14 % at the shipwreck. | 2026-11-14 | **The draw side's join point is the painter at the start of the next `fpcM_Management`**, not the end of the actor's draw: over half of what an actor's draw writes is read only there, so per-model jobs have the rest of the draw loop to finish. What they must privatize is the draw-side scratch (`j3dSys`, `J3DSys::mCurrentMtx/S/ParentS`, the clipper, the matrix stack, the GD display-list builder, `sNoUse*MtxPtr`, `sInterruptFlag`) and the per-type shared heap; what they must write back before the next execute pass is the cross-pass set (`j3dSys`, `roomControl::mStatus`, `l_fopDwTg_id`, `g_env_light`, `sTexCoordScaleTable`, the model matrices attachments read). The draw-buffer order (`entryNum`) stays serial |
